# /brag plan: Explain My Code (tone: `yc-parody`)

## Angle
A deadpan startup pitch deck, played completely straight. Each slide makes one claim with a numbered label ("01 — THE PROBLEM"), and hard cuts land on the beat. The humor comes from the project itself: a tautological "problem" slide, the tutor's calm "this line will never be reached due to the preceding crash", a proudly presented **0 KB**, and an "ask" that is just "Paste your code."

## Hook
0–2.5 s, `01 — THE PROBLEM`: **"Code is written in `code`."** The last word types in Geist Mono with a caret. It's absurd, true, and readable in about 1.5 s.

## Highlights
1. `02 — THE SOLUTION`: hard cut to the real landing hero ("Understand Any Code in Simple English") with the live GradientWaves shader.
2. `03 — THE PRODUCT`: "It explains every line." The real workspace: click Explain, then Line by Line, then zoom in on row 5's "this line will never be reached".
3. `04 — THE PRODUCT (CONT'D)`: "It also tells you why it crashed." The real bug card (ERROR · Line 3) and its Proposed Solution.
4. `05 — BY THE NUMBERS`: the site's own metrics, one hard cut per number: 25+ Languages Supported → < 1.5s Response Speed → 50 / day Free Tier Quota → **0 KB Zero Code Retained**.

## Punchline / outro
`06 — THE ASK`: "Paste your code.", then `explainmycode.dev` and "Free forever tier. No login required."

## Visual identity
- Pitch-deck chrome on the product's `#0c0418`: mono cyan section labels, a logo and name in the footer, and a page counter "0N / 06".
- Inter 800 statements with the site's cyan→violet→pink gradient on key words.
- Metrics in the site's own colors (white, cyan, fuchsia, emerald).
- Every UI frame is a DOM snapshot of the running app.

## Sound
Earnest "inspirational startup" piano at 96 BPM playing I–V–vi–IV in F, played straight. A soft kick enters at the product demo. Each slide cut gets a soft sub thud tuned to F. Number cuts climb C5 → E5 → F5 → G5 and resolve to A5 on the F chord at the ask.
